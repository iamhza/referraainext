## 🗂️ New Data Model Architecture

```
organizations (tenant)
    ↓
org_members (junction) ← NEW!
    ↓ userId
users (identity only)

org_members → providerId (for PROVIDER_USER role)
    ↓
providers

clients (nested PHI) ← ENHANCED!
    ↓ organizationId, caseManagerId
    identity: {firstName, lastName, dob, externalId}
    contact: {address, phone, email}
    bands: {language, accessibility}
    clinical: {primaryDiagnosis, mentalHealthNeeds}
    insurance: {type, provider, number}
    
service_relationships ← ENHANCED!
    ↓ clientId, providerId
    status: REFERRAL_SENT | PENDING_START | ACTIVE | PAUSED | CLOSED
    clientName, providerName (denormalized)
    flag, flagNote
    phiReleased tracking
    
authorizations ← NEW!
    ↓ serviceRelationshipId
    status: DRAFT | SUBMITTED | APPROVED | DENIED | EXPIRED
    startDate, endDate, units, approvalNumber
    
actions (simplified) ← SIMPLIFIED!
    ↓ subjectId (service_relationship, client, or referral)
    type: REQUEST_INTAKE | REQUEST_UPDATE | REQUEST_DOCUMENT | GENERAL_MESSAGE
    status: OPEN | COMPLETED | CANCELLED
    priority: NORMAL | HIGH | CRITICAL
    requestPayload, responsePayload
    createdByMemberId (org_members)
```

# Competitive Analysis: Making Referra 100x Better Than Current Solutions

## Current Provider Software Landscape

### What Providers Use Today (Major Players)

#### 1. **Procentive/Ensora Health** 
- **Focus**: Behavioral health EHR for mental health practices
- **Pain Points We Observed**:
  - Built for clinical practices, NOT social services referrals
  - No referral management between case managers and providers
  - Heavy documentation burden
  - No intelligent matching or AI assistance
  - Complex billing focus that doesn't apply to social services

#### 2. **Therap Services**
- **Focus**: Documentation and oversight for developmental disabilities
- **Pain Points We Observed**:
  - Person-centered planning tools but no referral coordination
  - No communication bridge between case managers and providers
  - Manual workflows throughout
  - Limited mobile access
  - No predictive intelligence

#### 3. **Casebook/Bonterra Apricot**
- **Focus**: Case management for nonprofits and public agencies
- **Pain Points We Observed**:
  - Internal case management only - no external referral coordination
  - No provider matching or selection tools
  - Manual reporting and compliance
  - No real-time collaboration features
  - Limited to single-organization workflows

#### 4. **FAMCare/Cumulus/CAP Systems**
- **Focus**: Human services case management
- **Pain Points We Observed**:
  - Agency-centric, not referral-centric
  - No cross-organizational communication
  - Heavy on forms, light on outcomes
  - No intelligent automation
  - Compliance-focused rather than relationship-focused

## What Case Managers Actually Do Daily (From Research)

### Thomas Allen & Similar Organizations (Minnesota Social Services)
- **Morning**: Review 20-40 client caseloads across multiple systems
- **Daily Hell**: Manually tracking which clients need which services
- **Workflow Breakdown**:
  1. Check MnCHOICES for assessments
  2. Review waiver authorizations (DD, CADI, BI, CAC, EW)
  3. Hunt for available providers via phone calls/spreadsheets
  4. Create referrals in separate systems
  5. Chase status updates via email/phone
  6. Update multiple documentation systems
  7. Generate compliance reports for DHS

### Brain Injury Alliance & Disability Organizations
- **Core Pain**: No visibility into provider capacity or client progress
- **Time Wasters**:
  - Calling 10+ providers to find availability
  - Re-explaining client needs to each provider
  - Missing service deadlines due to poor tracking
  - Duplicate documentation across systems
